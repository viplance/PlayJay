# Privacy Policy for PlayJay

Last updated: October 4, 2026

PlayJay is a local music player for macOS developed by Dzmitry Sharko.

## Data collection

PlayJay does not collect personal data or send data to the developer or third parties. The app contains no advertising, analytics, or tracking tools and does not require an account.

## Audio files and playback information

Audio files you choose are processed locally on your Mac. Your audio files are not uploaded or transmitted by PlayJay.

Track information and playback status are provided to macOS for system playback controls. This information is used locally to display and control playback.

## Changes to this policy

If PlayJay's privacy practices change, this policy will be updated to reflect those changes.

## Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer through [PlayJay's GitHub Issues page](https://github.com/viplance/PlayJay/issues). Issues are public, so please do not include sensitive personal information.
